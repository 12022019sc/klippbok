### Gaps Summary

No blocking gaps found. All 14 requirements are satisfied with substantive implementations and proper wiring.

One notable finding: MediaPipe Pose (pose_vector) is documented as one of the 7 signals but is not actually computed in `scorer.py`. The pose_vector field stays as an empty list, which means diversity selection operates on zero-vectors for the pose dimension. This does not block goal achievement since CLIP and face embeddings still drive diversity selection effectively, but it means the "7 signals" claim is technically 6 signals + pHash dedup (rather than 7 continuous signals). The score popover does not display pose_vector, so users won't see a missing signal.

All automated checks pass. Human verification is needed for the visual/interactive aspects listed above.
